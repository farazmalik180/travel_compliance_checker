ILLUSTRATIVE DEPARTURE COMPLIANCE RULES (author-written; not an official FIA document):

1. WORK VISAS:
- A valid passport with at least 6 months validity is required.
- A valid work visa for the destination country is mandatory.
- A Protector Stamp issued by the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment is legally mandatory on the passport for all Pakistani citizens departing on employment visas. Absence of the Protector Stamp requires immediate offloading.
- Return tickets and advance hotel bookings are not mandatory for work visas, as employment contracts typically provide employer accommodation and long-term residency.

2. VISIT & TOURIST VISAS:
- A valid passport with at least 6 months validity and a valid visa are required.
- A confirmed return flight ticket is mandatory.
- Confirmed advance hotel accommodation or registered host arrangement is mandatory.
- Sufficient financial proof (bank statement, international credit/debit card, or endorsed foreign currency) is mandatory.
- Departure must be denied (offload) if the traveler fails to present return flight proof, accommodation proof, or sufficient financial means.

3. BUSINESS & TRANSIT VISAS:
- Confirmed return ticket, proof of business purpose (vendor meetings, conference, trade fair), and sufficient financial backing are required.

4. MONITORED / HIGH-SCRUTINY TRANSIT HUBS:
- Travel to monitored destinations (e.g. Cambodia, UAE, Thailand, Malaysia, Iraq, Kenya, Kyrgyzstan, Qatar, Senegal, Libya) requires heightened vigilance to detect unauthorized labor migration masked under visit visas.
- Travelers heading to high-scrutiny destinations on visit visas must demonstrate clear intent to return and credible domestic anchorage.

5. WATCHLIST & IBMS ALERTS:
- Any traveler flagged with a Verified Watchlist Alert must be immediately offloaded for administrative investigation.
- Potential name matches require secondary documentation verification.
